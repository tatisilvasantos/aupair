🧡About Me🧡

Hi! I’m Tatiane Santos — but everyone calls me Tati! I’m a 25-year-old au pair from Curitiba, Brazil, currently living in Frisco, Texas. Calm, responsible, and deeply passionate about children, I bring warmth and intention to every moment I spend with them.

With more than 5,700 hours of childcare experience, I’ve had the joy of caring for children of all ages — from infants to school-aged — both in Brazil and the U.S. I’m Infant Qualified (I.Q.), fully vaccinated, a non-smoker, and pet-friendly. I speak English confidently, especially with children, and I love creating nurturing, engaging routines.

My childcare journey started early in Brazil, helping raise my younger brother and caring for an 8-month-old baby — a time that sparked my love for early childhood. In the U.S., I currently care for a 7-year-old boy and a 4-year-old girl, organizing creative play, keeping routines consistent, and filling our days with laughter, learning, and care.

Outside of childcare, I’m someone who finds joy in nature, animals, music, and creativity. I’m a Harry Potter fan, love to sing and dance with the kids, and have a deep appreciation for homemade cooking — meals made with love are my favorite way to connect.

I’m available for rematch through Cultural Care, and I’m searching for a kind, respectful family who’s looking for an au pair they can trust and rely on. I’m excited to contribute with love, structure, and genuine presence — and to create meaningful memories in your home.
